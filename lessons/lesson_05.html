HTML Headings

Organise your pages with six levels of headings from h1 to h6. In this lesson you will work through 2 phases. Complete each phase task before clicking Next Phase.

Phase 1
The Six Heading Levels
💡 Concept

HTML has six heading tags: <h1> through <h6>. h1 is the largest and most important — use it exactly once for the page's main title. h2 tags are major sections. h3 are sub-sections of h2, and so on. Headings are also important for SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) — they help Google understand what your page is about!

index.html 
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head><title>Headings</title></head>
  <body>

    <h1>Football — My Favourite Sport</h1>

      <h2>Famous Clubs</h2>
        <h3>Africa</h3>
          <h4>Nigeria</h4>
            <h5>Lagos</h5>
              <h6>My Local Team</h6>

      <h2>How to Play</h2>
        <h3>Rules</h3>
        <h3>Positions</h3>

  </body>
</html>

Phase 2
Headings and Search Engines
💡 Concept

Search engines like Google read your headings to understand your page. Your <h1> should describe the whole page. Your <h2> tags should name your major sections. Good headings also help people who use screen readers (software that reads pages aloud for people with visual impairments). Never use headings just to make text big — use them to show structure.

index.html 
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <title>How to Bake Bread</title>
  </head>
  <body>

    <h1>How to Bake Bread at Home</h1>
    <p>Fresh bread is delicious and easy to make!</p>

    <h2>Ingredients</h2>
    <p>You will need flour, water, salt and yeast.</p>

    <h2>Steps</h2>

    <h3>Step 1: Mix the Dough</h3>
    <p>Combine flour, water, salt and yeast in a bowl.</p>

    <h3>Step 2: Let it Rise</h3>
    <p>Cover the bowl and wait 1 hour.</p>

    <h3>Step 3: Bake</h3>
    <p>Put in the oven at 200°C for 30 minutes.</p>

    <h2>Tips</h2>
    <p>Use warm (not hot!) water to help the yeast.</p>

  </body>
</html>
✏️ Your Task

Write a page about a topic where each <h2> is a major section and each <h3> is a step or sub-topic. Make sure ALL your headings are in the right order — don't skip from h1 straight to h4!

🏗️ Build It!
My Topic Page with Headings

Build a well-structured page about any topic you choose, using h1 through h3 tags and paragraphs.

starter.html — copy this into a new file! 
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <title>[Your Topic] — A Complete Guide</title>
  </head>
  <body>

    <h1>[Your Topic Here]</h1>
    <p>Welcome to my page about [your topic]!
       I am going to tell you everything I know.</p>

    <h2>What Is [Your Topic]?</h2>
    <p>Write your introduction here. Explain what this topic is
       and why it is interesting.</p>

    <h2>Key Facts</h2>

    <h3>Fact 1 — [Title]</h3>
    <p>Write your first fact here.</p>

    <h3>Fact 2 — [Title]</h3>
    <p>Write your second fact here.</p>

    <h3>Fact 3 — [Title]</h3>
    <p>Write your third fact here.</p>

    <h2>Why I Love This Topic</h2>
    <p>Write about why this topic is special to you.</p>

    <hr>
    <p>Created by [Your Name] | Techbase HTML Course — Lesson 05</p>

  </body>
</html>
